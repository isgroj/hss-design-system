Joint Replacement

Advanced hip, knee, and shoulder replacement from the #1 hospital in orthopedics, with faster recovery and better outcomes.

Overview

HSS performs more joint replacement procedures than any other hospital in the country. Our surgeons use the latest techniques, including minimally invasive approaches, robotic-assisted surgery, and patient-specific implants to ensure the best possible outcomes.

We offer comprehensive care from initial evaluation through recovery, including pre-surgical education, personalized surgical planning, and dedicated rehabilitation programs designed to get you moving again as quickly and safely as possible.

Conditions We Treat

Our joint replacement specialists treat a wide range of conditions that affect the hip, knee, and shoulder joints.

  • Osteoarthritis
  • Rheumatoid Arthritis
  • Avascular Necrosis
  • Post-Traumatic Arthritis
  • Hip Dysplasia
  • Bone Fractures
  • Rotator Cuff Arthropathy
  • Failed Previous Surgery
  • Inflammatory Arthritis
  • Cartilage Damage

Our History

For over 160 years, HSS has been a pioneer in musculoskeletal health, advancing the science and practice of orthopedic surgery.

On This Page

  • Our Founding

  • Modern Era

Our Founding

Hospital for Special Surgery was founded in 1863 as the Hospital for the Ruptured and Crippled by Dr. James Knight. It was one of the first hospitals in the nation dedicated to orthopedic medicine, born from a conviction that patients with musculoskeletal conditions deserved specialized care.

From its earliest days, the institution was committed to both clinical excellence and medical education, establishing a teaching philosophy that continues to this day.

Our mission has always been simple: to help people move better so they can live better.

Dr. James Knight, Founder, 1863

Growth & Innovation

Throughout the 20th century, HSS became a world leader in orthopedic research and innovation. Our surgeons developed groundbreaking techniques in joint replacement, sports medicine, and rehabilitation that are now standard practice worldwide.

Photography: HSS in Action

Pioneering Joint Replacement

In the 1970s, HSS surgeons helped develop and refine total joint replacement procedures that have since restored mobility to millions of patients around the world.

By the Numbers

Today, HSS is the largest academic medical center focused on musculoskeletal health in the world.

Looking Forward

As we look to the future, HSS continues to invest in research, technology, and expanded access to care. Our commitment to innovation means exploring new frontiers in regenerative medicine, precision surgery, and digital health tools that will define the next chapter of musculoskeletal care.

With locations across the northeastern United States and partnerships around the world, we are bringing world-class orthopedic care closer to the patients who need it most.

Design Audit

Findings and recommendations from the design system audit of all marketing page layouts. Tracked by implementation status.

Implemented Not Implemented

Implemented Recommendations

Logo Corrected to Brand Spec

Logo mark updated to use official HSS Blue (#0081C8 / PANTONE 3005), Inter Black (weight 900), tight kerning (-0.2px), and square corners (no border-radius). Matches the January 2026 Brand Onesheet exactly.

All layouts • Header & Footer

Footer Social Icons Fixed

Replaced broken stroke-based SVG icons with fill-only SVG paths. Added fill: currentColor; stroke: none; to ensure consistent rendering across browsers.

All layouts • Footer

Mobile Responsiveness — Logo Overlap Fixed

Logo was overlapping nav text at smaller viewports. Resolved by properly constraining logo width, increasing nav gap, and hiding utility bar on mobile. Hamburger menu button shown below 768px.

All layouts • Header

Dropdown Caret Spacing Improved

Custom select dropdowns now use padding-right: 32px with caret positioned at right 12px center for comfortable spacing between text and arrow indicator.

Locations • Find a Doctor

Body Text Color Standardized

Inconsistent grays (#333, #444, #555, #666) consolidated to a tight, purposeful range: #333 (primary), #3d3d3d (secondary), #4a4a4a (tertiary). Improves visual cohesion and WCAG compliance.

All layouts

Border Radius Tokenized

All border-radius values now use CSS tokens (--radius-sm: 4px, --radius-md: 8px, --radius-lg: 12px) instead of hardcoded pixel values. Logo mark explicitly set to 0 per brand spec.

All layouts

WCAG Contrast — Hero Breadcrumbs

About page hero breadcrumb links had opacity 0.7 on white text over dark backgrounds, failing WCAG AA. Increased to 0.9 for minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio.

About / Content

Sticky Element Alignment

Sticky filter bars, sidebars, and map containers had misaligned top values after nav height change. Corrected to 64px (filters), 88px (sidebars), 130px (map) to account for header + filter bar stacking.

Locations • Find a Doctor • Service Detail

Responsive Grid Collapse

All multi-column grids collapse to single-column on mobile (768px). Location cards, doctor cards, article grids, and footer columns stack vertically. Sidebar layouts become full-width stacked sections.

All layouts

Not Yet Implemented

Interactive Map Integration

Locations page uses a static placeholder for the map panel. Needs integration with a mapping provider (Google Maps, Mapbox) for interactive pin markers, zoom, and click-to-select location behavior.

Locations • Priority: High

Functional Filter & Search Logic

Filter dropdowns and search inputs are styled but non-functional. Requires JavaScript to handle filtering, sorting, and live search across location cards, doctor cards, and article listings.

Locations • Find a Doctor • News/Blog • Priority: High

Mobile Navigation Drawer

Hamburger menu button is present on mobile but no slide-out drawer is implemented. Needs an off-canvas panel with all nav links, utility links (MyHSS, search), and smooth open/close animation.

All layouts • Priority: High

Pagination / Infinite Scroll

Location list, doctor results, and blog articles show static content with no pagination controls or "Load More" behavior. For production, implement paginated results with proper state management.

Locations • Find a Doctor • News/Blog • Priority: Medium

Doctor Star Ratings — Real Data

Star ratings on doctor cards are hardcoded. Need API integration to pull real patient satisfaction scores and review counts. Consider showing "No reviews yet" state and rating distribution tooltips.

Find a Doctor • Priority: Medium

Appointment Booking Form Validation

Sidebar appointment forms on Service Detail and Find a Doctor pages are layout-only. Need form validation, date picker integration, insurance provider lookup, and submission flow with confirmation state.

Service Detail • Find a Doctor • Priority: Medium

Table of Contents — Scroll Spy

About/Content page sidebar TOC is static. Needs JavaScript scroll spy to highlight the current section and smooth-scroll to anchors on click. Consider adding progress indicator.

About / Content • Priority: Medium

Physician Carousel Interaction

Service Detail page physician section shows a static grid. Convert to a horizontally scrollable carousel with prev/next controls, touch swipe support, and auto-rotation with pause-on-hover.

Service Detail • Priority: Low

Dark Mode Support

Marketing pages are light-only. The data design system supports dark mode with semantic tokens. Marketing layouts should adopt prefers-color-scheme: dark with adjusted brand colors and image treatments.

All layouts • Priority: Low

Skip-to-Content & ARIA Landmarks

No skip-to-content link or full ARIA landmark roles on layout regions. Add role="main", role="search", and a visible-on-focus skip link for keyboard/screen reader users.

All layouts • Priority: Medium

Performance — Image Lazy Loading

Doctor photos, article thumbnails, and hero images should use loading="lazy" and responsive srcset with WebP/AVIF fallbacks. Currently using placeholder backgrounds — real images will need optimization.

All layouts • Priority: Low

Print Stylesheet

No @media print styles defined. Service Detail and About pages particularly benefit from print optimization — hide nav, expand sidebars inline, ensure readable font sizes.

Service Detail • About / Content • Priority: Low
