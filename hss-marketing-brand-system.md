# HSS Marketing Brand System — Claude Reference

This file is the authoritative reference for all HSS **marketing and brand communications**. It extends the HSS Data Design System with marketing-specific guidelines sourced from the official HSS Brand Identity Guidelines (January 2026) and the HSS Brand Onesheet.

For **application UI** (dashboards, components, data-viz), see the HSS Data Design System skill (`hss-design-system`).

## Brand Foundation

To help people get back to what they need and love to do better than any other place in the world.

To provide the highest quality patient care, improve mobility and enhance the quality of life for all, and to advance the science of orthopedic surgery, rheumatology and their related disciplines through research and education.

Diversity, Excellence, Gratitude, Innovation, Integrity, Passion, Teamwork

## Tone of Voice

### Characteristics
| We Are | We Are Not |
|--------|------------|
| Knowledgeable | Boastful |
| Personable | Playful |
| Direct | Verbose |
| Approachable | Clinical |
| Confident | Alarming |

### Guardrails
1. **Speak with confidence.** Our expertise is assumed. No boasting. Reserved self-assurance backed by factual knowledge and industry leadership.
2. **Speak in simple terms.** Bridge the gap between medical specialists and the everyday person. Avoid jargon and technical words.
3. **Don't scaremonger.** Promote orthopedic health positively. Never intentionally make people anxious. Be proactive in how we communicate.

### Headline Rules
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Meet your local world-class Orthopedic specialists." | "The best Orthopedic care is now a hop, skip and a jump away." |
| "The #1 in orthopedic care is now in Stamford." | "Just your local, best in the world, Orthopedic center where you will find our top experts to help you." |

## Logo

### The HSS Monogram
- Always presented in **HSS Blue** (`#0074BC` digital / PMS 3005 print)
- The monogram represents the entire HSS ecosystem

### Clear Space
- Minimum clear space = **X** on all sides, where X = 1/4 the height of the monogram logo

### Minimum Size
- **0.5 inches** (36 pixels) in height — never smaller

### Background Control

#### Preferred Background
| Background | Notes |
|------------|-------|
| HSS Cool Neutral (`#F0F5F7`) | **Preferred** — logo in HSS Blue on neutral background |

#### Alternative Backgrounds
| Background | Notes |
|------------|-------|
| Light photograph | Logo in HSS Blue — background must be light and must not distract from the logo |
| HSS Blue (`#0074BC`) | Logo in HSS Blue with **white outline** — for use on HSS Blue backgrounds only |
| White (`#FFFFFF`) | Logo in HSS Blue on white |

The HSS logo should not appear on colors other than those shown above.

### Logo and Taglines

The following lockups are the only approved configurations. The same Clear Space, Minimum Size, and Background Control rules apply to lockups as to the HSS logo.

#### Approved Lockups
| Lockup | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| HSS + **HOSPITAL FOR SPECIAL SURGERY** | Full name lockup — used where "HSS" may not be well known or Hospital association is beneficial |
| HSS + **#1 IN ORTHOPEDICS** | Ranking lockup — HSS monogram with "#1 IN ORTHOPEDICS" text |
| HSS + **CHOOSE BETTER MOVE BETTER** | Tagline lockup — HSS monogram with brand tagline |
| HSS + **Lerner Children's Pavilion** | Pediatric unit lockup — dedicated to the care of growing kids. Available in horizontal and vertical logo versions. Should always be used consistently. |

#### Our Tagline
- "Choose Better. Move Better." — always ALL CAPS, HSS Blue
- Standard usage is in all caps and HSS Blue
- Other variations are acceptable with approval from Marketing
- Artwork is provided and should always be used to ensure consistent presentation — available from the HSS Brand Center on the intranet

#### Positive vs. Reverse
- **Positive lockups** (HSS Blue on light background) — **preferred**
- **Reverse lockups** (white on HSS Blue background) — for use on dark backgrounds only
- The positive lockups on a light background is preferred

### Incorrect Logo Use
- Never use old versions of the logo
- Never change the color of the logo
- Never add text or graphic elements to the logo
- Never distort the logo
- Never use the outline logo over a photograph
- Never use in black when color is possible (exception: B&W advertising)

The business is divided into different areas: business-to-business (B2B), consumer-facing, or a combination of the two.

- **HSS** Research Institute

### Consumer Facing Patient Care

## Business Descriptors

Business descriptors define individual business areas. They are **never locked up with the HSS logo** — always presented separately in brand typeface.

### Format
- **"HSS"** in Inter Black
- **Business area** in Inter Medium
- Always shown in HSS Blue on white backgrounds, or reversed to white on dark backgrounds

### Examples
- HSS Education Institute
- HSS Research Institute
- HSS Innovation Institute
- HSS Perform

### Hospital for Special Surgery Descriptor
- Uses a vertical line separator: **HSS | Hospital for Special Surgery**
- The vertical line must always be present when used as a graphic lockup
- Not required when writing in body text

### Location Descriptors
- Branding for locations off the main campus varies depending on the location and the relationship HSS has with affiliates
- Wholly owned locations: identified by city/location name (e.g., "HSS Westchester")
- Location name can lock up with HSS logo **for signage only**
- All other marketing materials: HSS monogram presented as the hero, location name in text only appears separately
- As HSS expands, nomenclature evolves — contact **brandguidelinequestions@hss.edu** for logo questions

#### Location Examples
| Location | Type |
|----------|------|
| HSS Westchester | Wholly owned — signage lockup allowed |
| Northern NJ Surgery Center | Affiliate — HSS monogram + name in text |
| HSS Paramus, Midland Ave. | Wholly owned with street address |

### Departments & Services

HSS departments and services that produce public-facing materials may use a descriptor lock-up similar to the business descriptor.

- The department or service is presented in **Inter Medium** following **HSS** in **Inter Black**
- Always shown in HSS Blue
- On business communications (e.g., business cards), the department is noted as part of the title or description. The department or service is part of Hospital for Special Surgery, which should be used in the address line
- On consumer-facing communications, the department or service is in plain text in the body copy, and the descriptor lock-up is used as a sign-off

#### Department & Service Examples
- **HSS** | Hospital for Special Surgery
- **HSS** Spine
- **HSS** Sports Medicine Institute
- **HSS** Adult Reconstruction and Joint Replacement

### Special Programs

Programs and offerings that have been named for a donor or other honoree may have a special lock-up with the HSS logo.

- On occasion, logo lock-ups may be combined with programs that have unique names (e.g., Lerner Children's Pavilion + Penny Doerge Adaptive Academy)
- **HSS Perform** is an offering direct to employers (B2B)
- Internal programs offered to HSS staff may also be branded with permission from HSS Marketing (e.g., **Stronger with HSS** — the wellness and resilience program)
- Employee Resource Groups are covered in the ERG section

### Incorrect Descriptor Use
- Never lock up business/service name with the HSS logo (exception: signage)
- Never change font weights (HSS = Inter Black, descriptor = Inter Medium)
- Never remove the vertical line from "HSS | Hospital for Special Surgery"
- Never use ALL CAPITALS for the business/service or city name
- Never present the descriptor in any color other than HSS Blue
- Never present the descriptor without "HSS" prefix (in graphic usage)

## Typography

### Brand Typefaces
| Family | Usage | Weights |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **Inter** | Primary brand typeface for all HSS materials | Regular, Regular Italic, Medium, Medium Italic, SemiBold, SemiBold Italic, Bold, Bold Italic, ExtraBold, ExtraBold Italic, Black, Black Italic |
| **Sofia Sans Condensed** | Condensed applications: legal copy, footnotes, boilerplate | Regular, Bold |
| **Arial** | Fallback for Microsoft applications (PowerPoint, Word) only | — |

### Print Typographic Hierarchy
| Role | Font | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Primary headline | Inter Bold, in HSS Blue | Large and prominent |
| Secondary headline / intro paragraph | Inter Medium | — |
| Subhead / text highlight | Inter Medium or Bold | — |
| Body copy | Inter Regular | — |
| Boilerplate / legal | Sofia Sans Condensed Italic or Book | Small footnote text |

### Typographic Style Rules
- Headlines for branded collateral: upper and lower case OR all caps
- Headlines for advertising: always ALL CAPS
- All caps can be combined with upper & lower case to separate information
- **#1 statement** ("#1 IN ORTHOPEDICS"): always HSS Blue or white, always ALL CAPS
- Prioritize HSS Blue for type, followed by HSS Dark Blue, then Black for variety
- Bold typography combined with large numbers creates distinctive design elements
- Large numbers are effective at calling attention to details — combine large, bold numbers and typography to create a distinctive table of contents
- Large quotation marks create a distinctive design element with quotes
- Note: print typographic style guide applies to print only — digital applications require ADA compliance and have different specifications (see below)

### Digital Typography — ADA Compliance
| Color | On White Background | Reversed on Dark |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|
| HSS ADA Blue (`#0074BC`) | Min 12pt regular | Min 12pt regular |
| HSS Dark Blue (`#264583`) | Min 12pt regular | Min 12pt regular |
| HSS Blue (`#0077C8` for graphics) | Min 18pt bold | Min 18pt bold |
| HSS ADA Green (`#68A42D`) | Min 18pt bold | Min 18pt bold |
| HSS ADA Teal (`#00A4AF`) | Min 18pt bold | Min 18pt bold |
| HSS Dark Gray (`#5F849C`) | Min 18pt bold | Min 18pt bold |
| HSS Gold (`#D4B640`) | **Not compliant on white** | Min 12pt bold (reversed only) |

### Non-Compliant Colors for Text
These colors must **never** be used for text (graphics/icons only):
- HSS Cyan (`#00ADE6`)
- HSS Teal (`#00AEBA` — print/brand version)
- HSS Cool Neutral (`#F0F5F7`)
- HSS Green (`#72B431` — print version)
- HSS Light Gray (`#A2BACA`)
- HSS Warm Neutral (`#F5F5F5`)

## Co-Branding

### Co-branded Collaborations
Defined as overt patient care offerings through relationships with third-party providers that meet HSS clinical standards but are not wholly a legally binding structure, such as joint venture.

| Relationship Type | Language | Example |
|-------------------|----------|---------|
| Collaboration | "in collaboration with" or "with" | HSS Orthopedics in collaboration with Stamford Health |
| Collaboration | "in collaboration with" | HSS in collaboration with Lima Corporate |
| At-location | "at" | HSS at NCH |
| Knowledge/data contribution | "Powered by HSS" | RightMove, CarePathways |

**Never use "partnership"** — does not meet legal definition (which has important risk and liability implications).

### Co-branded Logo Rules
- Size parity: approximate area (height x width) of HSS monogram = approximate area of co-brand logo
- A vertical line, equal in height to the HSS monogram, separates the two logos
- Width of the H in HSS used as spacing guide between logos and line separator
- Minimum size: 0.5" (36 pixels)
- Subject to prior approval by HSS Legal and HSS Brand Management

## Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)

### ERG Logos
Each group has a unique logo locked up with the HSS monogram and descriptor line.

| ERG | Name |
|-----|------|
| BEST | Black Employees Standing Together |
| HOLA | Hispanic Organization for Leadership & Advancement |
| WISE | Women Inspiring Success & Excellence |
| HAPI | HSS Asian & Pacific Islanders |
| LGBTQ+ | LGBTQ+ Standing Together |

### ERG Usage Rules
- Primarily for **internal use** (meetings, campus signage, internal comms)
- Should **not** be used externally to represent the organization as a whole
- **Exception:** parades, promotional events, or sponsorships specific to diverse communities
- Should **not** be used in email signatures
- Preferred backgrounds: White and Black
- Clear space and minimum size based on HSS monogram height (1/4 height = X)
- BEST logo on black background: black stripe replaced with lighter green for legibility

### ERG Design Rules
- ERG logo colors are reserved for the logos only — never incorporated into design elements
- Follow standard HSS brand guidelines for typography, color, and imagery
- All caps NOT required for ERG headlines — use standard HSS typographic hierarchy

### ERG Incorrect Use
- Never rearrange logo elements
- Never change logo colors
- Never add graphic elements to the logo
- Never use colors or design elements outside HSS brand guidelines

## Design Highlights

Key design principles across all collateral types:
- The logo can be locked up with the tagline in advertising and marketing materials
- Headlines are large and bold — for branded collateral, upper and lower case or all caps can be used
- Headlines for advertising should be in all caps
- Photography depicts real life, used purposefully to add value to the message
- Backgrounds can be HSS Cool Neutral in color; headlines for advertising should be in all caps
- Secondary colors can be used to separate areas of information
- The HSS logo is always presented prominently
- Bold use of typography is encouraged — large Inter Bold headlines in HSS Blue
- Printed brochures should feature bold use of typography with the HSS logo always in a prominent position

## Marketing Collateral Patterns

### Flyers
- Color focus: HSS Blue
- HSS logo presented prominently
- Bold typography (Inter Bold for headlines in HSS Blue)
- Relatable photography
- ERG logo anchored in lower left for ERG-related flyers

### Pocket Brochures
- Can have 1, 2, or 3 panels
- Follow standard HSS brand typography and color

### Postcards
- Can be mailed or used as handouts
- Multiple purposes, all using brand elements

### Business Cards
- Specific layouts depending on information volume
- Standard, 3-line title, 4-5 line title, and QR code variants
- Must use approved templates — never recreate layouts
- Order through Linear/GHX e-requisitions

### Stationery
- Specific layouts — never alter
- Must use approved templates
- Order through approved sources

### Presentations (PowerPoint)
- Templates available in widescreen and letter size
- Include correct logos, color palettes, and sample imagery
- Available via Templafy in PowerPoint or Brand Center
- Font fallback: Arial (when Inter unavailable)

### Email Newsletters
- HSS logo presented prominently
- Headlines: large and bold
- Photography used purposefully to add value to the message
- Secondary colors can separate areas of information

### Branded Merchandise
- Only HSS monogram logo (no department branding)
- Must follow logo rules from this guide
- Contact brandguidelinequestions@hss.edu for assistance
